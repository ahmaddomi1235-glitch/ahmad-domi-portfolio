## 1. Method and access notes

- src-004 read in full with PyMuPDF (40 pages, page 9 is blank), NFKC-normalised. **Pitfall:** the PDF stores the lam-alef ligature reversed, so «لا» comes out as «ال» (e.g. «ال يجوز» for «لا يجوز»; «أوال» for «أولًا»). Quotes below were repaired by hand against meaning. Page numbers are PDF pages and equal the printed numbers.
- Every page of src-004 carries a header saying its examples must not be reused. All of the PDF's own worked material (phishing/financial firm, laptops/field technicians, computer vision/factory) was left alone.
- src-005 was **not opened**. src-020 was not opened as a document; while checking what to exclude I printed about 40 short heading lines of the repo's pre-extracted text of src-020 and saw criterion-coded task headings, a KPI line and a context/needs/objective/scope/structure introduction skeleton. Nothing from it was used. The exclusion list also relies on Agent 05's report (`operations/domi-recommendation-engine/reports/agent05_library_and_e2_draft.md`).
- For the library check (section 4) I extracted page text of eight library PDFs into the session scratchpad (outside the repo) to read them. They are temporary and deleted at the end of this run; no library file was copied into the repo.
- Registry note: the base registry labels src-004 and src-069 `PUBLIC_SUMMARY_CANDIDATE`; `overrides.json` later re-labels both `PUBLIC_DERIVED_PUBLISHED`. I treated "PUBLIC_PUBLISHED or PUBLIC_SUMMARY_CANDIDATE" literally: the read set was src-001, 017, 033, 043, 064, 066, 099 (PUBLIC_PUBLISHED) plus src-004 and src-069. Files labelled PUBLIC_CANDIDATE or PUBLIC_DERIVED_PUBLISHED (including src-015, the source the threat-analysis page cites) were **not read**; only keyword counts from the repo's own extracts were used to confirm the absence of approach content.
