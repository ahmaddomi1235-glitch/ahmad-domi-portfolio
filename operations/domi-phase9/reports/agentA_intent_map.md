## 0. How to read this report

**Prompt set.** 50 prompts: all 20 frozen Phase 8 questions (Q01-Q20, wording untouched, from `operations/geo-mission/prompts_phase8_assignment_20_v1.json`), 9 of the 14 prompts of `operations/geo-mission/prompts_assignment_help_v1.json` that are genuine assignment-help or definition questions (AI1, AI2, CY1, CY2, GH1, GH2, GH3, P033, P037; wording untouched), and 21 new prompts N21-N41. Not reused from v1: P061, P069, P057, P049, P001 (resource or teacher-finding intent, not assignment help). Every prompt was checked by script: none contains Ahmad, أحمد, دومي, Domi or a domain name. New prompts are written in the Levantine/Jordanian style with English terms (Assignment, Merit, Evaluate, Bias, Firewall...).

**Coverage rubric (judged by reading the page bodies, not titles or the search index).** The site's concept pages are short (median about 235 words, roughly 1000 to 2000 characters of body) knowledge pages in Arabic with English terms.
- COVERED: a page answers the core ask with enough stepwise content that a student could act on it (a method, a table, a worked mini-example, or a "how to justify" instruction).
- PARTIAL: the concepts are explained but the method asked for (justify, evaluate, analyse a given artefact, map to criteria, review own text) is missing or is a single line.
- GAP: nothing on the site addresses the intent (confirmed by searching content/ for the key terms).
This is stricter than `docs/ASSIGNMENT_RETRIEVAL_GAPS.md` in four places: Q13 and Q16 move COVERED to PARTIAL (the explain/analyse half is missing), Q06 moves PARTIAL to COVERED (defence-in-depth has an explicit "how to write the recommendation" section), and Q07/Q08/Q18 move GAP to PARTIAL because E2 added a self-review checklist on 2026-10-12.

**"Outside help genuinely useful?"** YES = the student's need is a judgement on their own work, brief, teacher feedback or a centre/awarding-body policy that a generic assistant cannot see. MAYBE = the generic method fully works but exam-board precision or the student's actual scenario/artefact decides the outcome. NO = a direct generic answer fully satisfies the student. This is stricter than the Phase 8 decision matrix in `docs/ASSIGNMENT_AI_BEHAVIOR_AUDIT.md` (that matrix says YES for Q04 and Q09 and MAYBE for Q06, Q12, Q15; here Q04 and Q09 are MAYBE, Q06 and Q12 are NO).

### 5.3 Edits that look safe but change a protected page

1. **Adding, removing, renaming or reordering any concept page in the AI or cyber unit.** The hub renders every concept card (title and summary), the "start here" trio, a question sample (every n-th concept's first `questionsAnswered`), the unit's glossary terms, its videos (`videos.json` `unitId`), its resources (`content/resources/materials.json`) and a JSON-LD ItemList. Changing a concept's `title_ar`, `summary`, `order` or first `questionsAnswered`, or any glossary, video or resource entry for those two units, rewrites the hub HTML. Editing only a concept page's body is safe for the hubs.
2. **Any new page that lists a protected ID in its own `related`.** Backlinks are derived (`linkedFrom`), so the protected page would start showing the new page in its related cards without any edit to the protected file.
3. **The shared templates**: `[unit]/page.tsx` (both E1 hubs plus the E2-linking helper array) and `[unit]/[concept]/page.tsx`, `Blocks`, `jsonld.ts`, `seo/intent.ts`, `SiteFooter`.
4. **`data/seo-intent-map.json`** entries for the four paths (title, description, H1 are generated from `scripts/seo/seo_copy.py` by `scripts/seo/build_intent_map.py`). Regenerating the whole map rewrites all entries; compare the four before committing.
5. **`lastReviewed`** on any of the four files or their linkers (feeds sitemap `lastmod` and JSON-LD `dateModified`).
6. A new page of any kind needs an entry in the intent map or `seoMeta` throws at build time (`No SEO entry for <path>`); adding entries for new paths does not touch the four pages.
